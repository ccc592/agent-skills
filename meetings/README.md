# Meetings
Purposeful meetings, accessible participation, and records you can trust.

## Why Install This Skill
Meetings often start before anyone has settled what needs deciding or who needs
to contribute. This skill helps your agent choose a meeting or async approach,
explain invitee choices, and design useful preparation and participation.

Afterward, it turns rough notes into readable decisions, dissent, agreed actions,
and open questions. Suggestions for the organizer stay separate from the shared
record. It offers accessibility options to everyone, adds individual support without
requiring a diagnosis. It can retain practical preferences through authorized
private memory and apply them to future meetings with that person. It never
treats a draft as permission to send invitations or create tasks.

## Quick Start
Ask: “Plan a decision meeting about the support rota, explain who needs to attend,
and draft a brief without sending invitations.” Or provide notes and ask for a
shared record plus separate private organizer suggestions.

## Triggers
- Decide whether a meeting is needed; plan its purpose and invitees.
- Facilitate accessible in-person, remote, or hybrid discussion.
- Convert notes or a transcript into a trustworthy meeting record.
- Prepare agreed-action tracking and private follow-up suggestions.

## Requirements
No scripts, credentials, or integrations are required. Calendar, colleague,
recording, task tools, and preference memory are optional and require their
normal access and user authorization. Without authorized memory, the agent states
that it cannot persist preferences. Works with existing organizational meeting and document practices.
